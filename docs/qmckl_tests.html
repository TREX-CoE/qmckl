Data for Tests

1. Introduction

The test programs use two molecules, CHBrClF and N2. This page documents the data they rely on: geometry, basis set, molecular-orbital coefficients, electron positions and Jastrow parameters. The same arrays are available to the C examples in the headers include/chbrclf.h and include/n2.h.

2. CHBrClF

This test is the all-electron Hartree-Fock wave function of CHBrClF, in the aug-cc-pVTZ basis set. This is a non-symmetric molecule made of five atoms, heavy and light. The aug-cc-pVTZ basis set has both diffuse and compact Gaussians, with large and small contractions, and with a high maximum angular momentum.

2.3. Molecular orbitals

There are 224 molecular orbitals and 263 basis functions, so the coefficient array holds 58912 numbers. They are not reproduced here: the block below only declares the array, and the full table is in the header include/chbrclf.h.

2.4. Electron coordinates

Electron coordinates are stored in atomic units in normal format, one row per electron. The array holds two walkers of 68 electrons, 34 up and 34 down.

3. N2

This data set is mainly for the Jastrow factor. Ramón Panadés-Barrueta supplied the coordinates and the Jastrow coefficients, taken from his fork of IRPJast. The two core electrons of each nitrogen atom are described by pseudopotentials and are left out of the calculation.

Number of atoms 2
Number of alpha electrons 5
Number of beta electrons 5
Number of core electrons 4

3.1. XYZ coordinates

The geometry is given below in XYZ format; the coordinates are in angstrom.

Nuclear coordinates are stored in atomic units in transposed format.

3.2. Electron coordinates

Electron coordinates are stored in atomic units in normal format, one row per electron. The array holds two walkers of 10 electrons, 5 up and 5 down.

3.3. Jastrow related data

The two nitrogen atoms share a single nucleus type, so n2_type_nucl_vector is zero for both. n2_a_vector, n2_b_vector and n2_c_vector hold the coefficients of the electron-nucleus, electron-electron and electron-electron-nucleus terms of the Jastrow factor. n2_c_vector stores one column per nucleus type, n2_c_vector_full repeats it for each nucleus, and n2_lkpm_of_cindex gives the combined l, k, p and m indices of each c coefficient.
